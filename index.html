What is the duration of the BBA in FinTech?

The programme is three years, with a fourth-year Honours with Research option available under NEP 2020.

3 Years Standard 4-Year Honours with Research NEP 2020 Aligned

What core technology areas are covered in this programme?

The curriculum covers Blockchain, Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Mobile Computing, and digital payment systems.

Blockchain Artificial Intelligence Cloud & Mobile Computing Digital Payments

Are internships part of the BBA FinTech curriculum?

Yes — internships with top companies and an industry-relevant syllabus are fully integrated into the programme.

Top BFSI Partners Integrated Internships Real-World Experience

What are the NEP 2020 exit options available?

Students can exit with a Certificate (1 year), UG Diploma (2 years), BBA Degree (3 years), or Honours with Research (4 years).

1-Yr Certificate 2-Yr UG Diploma 3-Yr BBA Degree 4-Yr Honours

PayTech Expert, Blockchain Developer, Financial Analyst, Risk and Compliance Analyst, Data Analyst in Financial Services, and more.
